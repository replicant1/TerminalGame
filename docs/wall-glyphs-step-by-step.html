The maze knows only which cells are open and which are wall. It has no idea what a corner is. Press Step to walk the arena cell by cell, in the order _to_layers walks it, and watch each solid block of wall ask its four questions and turn into the line that answers them. The two layers. One walk fills both. A wall cell puts a glyph in the wall layer and leaves the pill layer blank there; a corridor cell does the opposite. Each layer is blank wherever the other has something, which is what lets the screen draw them in two passes and give the pills a different colour from the walls they sit between.

The right-hand character. A cell is two characters wide and the glyph sits in the left one. The right one carries a dash only when the wall really continues east. Filling it in any other case would leave the wall touching the pill in the next cell, with none of the gap every other wall cell leaves.

Out of bounds counts as not-wall. A cell on the edge of the arena has neighbours that do not exist. Treating them as wall would give the border arms pointing off the edge of the picture; treating them as open closes the border into a rectangle. Watch the corners of row 0 to see it happen.
